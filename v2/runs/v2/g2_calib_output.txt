8. OUTCOME OF THE G2 CALIBRATION
========================================================================
  NEGATIVE CALIBRATION RESULT. Two independent grounds:

    (a) No configuration passes the frozen safety screen.
    (b) The primary endpoint is degenerate: safe goal completion
        is 0.000 for every arm and every configuration.

  Per the frozen protocol and the Amendment F precedent, the
  150-scenario EVALUATION POOL IS NOT LAUNCHED. It remains
  untouched and geometrically validated.

  The screen must NOT be relaxed and the pool must NOT be
  regenerated to yield safe completions. Selecting scenario
  difficulty to produce a desired outcome is the error that
  retired Amendment G.
