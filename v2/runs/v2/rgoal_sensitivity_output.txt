  CONCLUSION
  The comparison is INSENSITIVE to the goal radius across every
  physically sensible value. SFM-only stays at 33% and the hybrid at
  100% from the frozen 2 m out to 10 m, because the nearest SFM
  failure terminates 14.08 m from the goal -- SFM failures are not
  near misses, they are gross failures.

  The curve only moves once the radius exceeds 14 m, which is 7x the
  frozen value and 20% of the 69.3 m arena diagonal -- a radius at
  which 'reaching the goal' has no meaning. Even at 30 m, SFM-only
  does not reach the hybrid arm.

  The 2 m threshold is therefore a reporting convention, not a
  result-bearing choice. Reviewer 1.13 is answered without re-running.
